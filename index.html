Everything you need to listen

A customizable Android music experience built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

🎧

Flexible playback

Crossfade, gapless, skip silence, tempo and pitch, equalizer, sleep timer and offline downloads.

📝

Synced lyrics

Follow lyrics from multiple sources with several display styles and translation support.

🤝

Listen Together

Real-time rooms with instant join, shared queue and live chat.

🔎

Echo Find

Recognize songs playing around you and add them to your library.

🪟

Liquid Glass UI

Material 3 with glass effects, ambient mode, themes, fonts and custom launcher icons.

🔗

Integrations

Last.fm, ListenBrainz, Spotify import, Discord Rich Presence and Google Cast on the GMS build.
